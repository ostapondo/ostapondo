Where this wins: it is instantly warm, and the emoji do real work in the “three ways” list, where three inputs need three marks. Where it wears thin: ☕ and 🪶 and 🧰 are decoration standing in for an idea, and the same set of emoji is on a thousand other landing pages — so the page ends up looking like everyone else's, which is the opposite of a brand. Variant B replaces every one of them with something only Plonk could use.
