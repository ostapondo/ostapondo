The five decisions that make it one thing rather than two. The cube keeps its black outline and flat fills, and nothing else on the page is allowed either — so it reads as the one solid object in a soft room. The mesh is mixed from its three face colours. It stands in an ambient glow whose hue drifts with the mesh, which is what stops a flat drawing from looking pasted onto a gradient. It overlaps the top edge of the glass instead of sitting inside it. And the window it is holding is drawn with the same 2.5 px outline as the cube — so the thing being carried belongs to the character, while the zones it lands in belong to the colour.

What I'd still watch. Glass over a moving mesh is the most expensive thing here on an old Mac in Safari, and it is the first thing I would drop to a flat translucent white if the page ever feels heavy. The mesh also has to stay quiet enough that the coloured zones still read as the loudest thing on screen — the moment the background competes with the product graphic, the page stops being about windows.
