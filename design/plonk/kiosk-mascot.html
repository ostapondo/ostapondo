Three ways

Whichever your hands prefer.

Drag it

The zones light up while the window is in your hand. Hold ⌘ and it takes two at once.

Press a key

Nine zones live on the digits. Zero is the oops key — it puts a window back exactly where it was.

Say it

Hold ⌃⌥V and name the place. It listens on the Mac, offline. Nobody hears you but the laptop.

Why I'd pick this one. The face does everything an emoji did, but it is yours — nobody else's landing page can use it, and it turns up again in the menu bar icon, the empty states, the first-run screen and the 404. And one joke does real work: on the privacy card the cube has its eyes shut, which is the argument and the charm in the same drawing. The cost is honest, though: someone has to draw five faces properly, and a mascot that is drawn badly is worse than no mascot.
