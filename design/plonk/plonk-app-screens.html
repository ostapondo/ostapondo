14 First launch

The only screen every single user sees. Two permissions, and it says what each one is actually for.

Two
permissions.

Accessibility

To move and resize other apps' windows. This is the whole job

Granted
Screen Recording

Only for capture, OCR and the ruler. Asked for the first time you use one

Grant
Everything else

No Full Disk Access, no Automation, no Keychain, no account

Not needed

This is the screen worth designing hardest. It is where a window manager loses people — an unexplained macOS permission sheet, then a relaunch. Saying what each grant buys, and that the rest is not asked for, is the whole pitch of the privacy page delivered at the moment it matters.

· Notes

Colour does one job in the app and a different one on the site. On the site it separates sections. Here it marks a mode: violet is placing, coral is editing, amber is awake, blue is capture. A user who has seen the site already knows the code.

Everything above is HTML and CSS, so it is a mockup, not a build. In SwiftUI the parts that carry over directly are the palette, the 28/16 px radii, the 900-weight page titles and the settings-row-as-tile. The animations are the app's real behaviour, not page decoration — the snap, the flash and the set swap already exist, they are just not shown anywhere.
