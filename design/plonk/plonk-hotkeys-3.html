03 Mono

The groups stay, but become labels over a table. Key and meaning are two columns that never move. The screen map is labelled with fractions, as everything is in this direction.

What works: the whole reference fits in one or two phone screens, a combination is found by eye in a second, and the key column aligns itself because the face is monospaced. What hurts: it looks like documentation rather than a product — after a loud front page the transition feels abrupt.

· Verdict

The three directions win and lose in different places, and that is not a problem with the choice — it is a hint that the pages are not the same kind of thing.

The most practical split is 01 on the front page, 03 on the reference pages, sharing one palette and one type scale so the move between them never reads as a different site. That is not a compromise: monobank does the same — the front page shouts in colour, the terms and the tariffs are set quietly and tight.
